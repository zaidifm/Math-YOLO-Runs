Let `X_m` be the chosen-simple-root incidence variety for degree-`m` binary forms whose `A^{m-1}B` coefficient is normalized to one.

On the finite-root chart `B=1`, the root equation eliminates the constant coefficient and the root derivative `D=f'(a)` replaces the next coefficient. Thus

\[
U_0\cong\operatorname{Spec}C[a,c_0,c_2,\ldots,c_{m-2},D,D^{-1}],
\]
so `Pic(U_0)=0` and the nonconstant units are powers of `D`.

On the infinity-root chart `A=1`, with `t=B/A`, the root equation eliminates `c_0` and

\[
E=1+2c_2t+3c_3t^2+\cdots+mc_m t^{m-1}
\]
is the root derivative. Hence

\[
U_\infty=\operatorname{Spec}C[t,c_2,\ldots,c_m,E^{-1}],
\]
again a UFD localization, with `Pic(U_infty)=0` and nonconstant units powers of `E`.

On the overlap `t!=0`, exact chain-rule computation gives

\[
\boxed{E=-t^{m-2}D.}
\]

Therefore overlap units have exponent lattice `Z<t> + Z<E>`, while chart-unit sublattices are generated by

\[
(0,1),\qquad (-(m-2),1).
\]

The Cech exact sequence for `O^*` gives

\[
\operatorname{Pic}(X_m)
\cong
\frac{Z^2}{\langle(0,1),(-(m-2),1)\rangle}
\cong
\boxed{Z/(m-2)Z}.
\]

Consequences:

- `m=3`: the Picard obstruction vanishes, and the earlier explicit chart calculation proves the stronger statement `X_3 ~= A^3`;
- every `m>3`: `Pic(X_m)` has nontrivial torsion, so `X_m` cannot be affine space.

Thus the normalized universal binary-form simple-root incidence recipe yields affine space only in the cubic case. Higher-degree Keller families necessarily require nonlinear coefficient slices and/or additional boundary normalization.
